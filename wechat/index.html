How to Set Up WeChat as a Foreigner

WeChat is not just a messaging app — it's how China works. Restaurants, taxis, shops, hotels, and even tourist attractions use WeChat for everything. Without it, life in China gets complicated fast. The good news: setup takes 10 minutes and works perfectly with your international phone number.

⏱ 10 min setup📅 Updated August 2026✅ No Chinese number needed

💡 Why WeChat is Different from WhatsApp
WeChat has mini-programs — tiny apps inside WeChat. You can order food, book transport, pay bills, and access hundreds of services without downloading separate apps. Think of it as a smartphone inside your smartphone, specific to China.

1

Download WeChat

  1. 1Open App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android)
  2. 2Search "WeChat" — the green icon with two chat bubbles
  3. 3Tap Get / Install and wait for download (~230MB)
  4. 4Open the app — you'll see the login screen
💡 Download at Home
WeChat is available internationally but download speeds are faster at home. Don't wait until you're at the airport.

2

Register Your Account

  1. 1Tap "Sign Up" on the welcome screen
  2. 2Enter your name (this is your display name — you can change it later)
  3. 3Select your country code and enter your phone number
  4. 4Tap "Send SMS" and enter the verification code
  5. 5Set a password (at least 8 characters with letters and numbers)
  6. 6Agree to the terms and complete the puzzle verification
⚠️ Use a number you'll always have access to
Your phone number is the key to login in your WeChat. If you lose access to that number, recovering your account is very difficult. Use your permanent home number, not a temporary travel SIM.

Get Verified by a Friend

This is the step most guides don't warn you about. New WeChat accounts may require verification by an existing WeChat user. This is WeChat's security measure against spam accounts.

  1. 1After registration, WeChat will ask you to have a friend verify you
  2. 2Ask a friend or colleague who has WeChat to scan your QR code
  3. 3They verify your account through their app — takes 10 seconds
  4. 4Your account is now fully active
💡 No Friends on WeChat Yet?
Ask in Facebook or Reddit travel groups — the China travel community is very helpful. Or message me — I'm happy to verify new accounts for Ready? China! readers.

4

Set Up WeChat Pay

  1. 1Tap Me → Pay → Wallet
  2. 2Tap "Bank Cards" → "Add a Card"
  3. 3Select your card type (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
  4. 4Enter your card number, expiry, and CVV
  5. 5Verify with SMS from your bank
  6. 6Set a 6-digit payment PIN
💳 Supported Cards
Visa and Mastercard work best. Tell your bank you're travelling to China before linking — some banks flag the WeChat verification as suspicious and temporarily block the card.
ℹ️ Alipay vs WeChat Pay
Both work almost everywhere in China. Most travelers use Alipay as their primary payment and WeChat Pay as backup. Set up both if you have time — it takes 20 minutes total. → Alipay setup guide

Essential WeChat Features to Know

🔍 Scan QR Codes
  • Tap + (top right) → Scan
  • Used for payments, adding contacts, restaurant menus
  • NOT the same as your phone's camera app
  • → Full QR scanning guide
🗺️ Mini Programs
  • Search any service in WeChat's search bar
  • Order food: Meituan mini-program
  • Book trains: 同程 mini-program
  • No extra downloads needed
💬 Translation
  • Long-press any message → Translate
  • Works for Chinese → English instantly
  • Useful for restaurant menus sent via WeChat
  • Also works in group chats
📍 Share Location
  • Tap + → Location → Share Real-time Location
  • Essential for meeting local friends or guides
  • Works without Google Maps
  • Pin drops in WeChat use Amap data (accurate in China)
