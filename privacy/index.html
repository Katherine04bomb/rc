Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, what data this site collects, which outside services it uses, and how affiliate links work. Ready? China! is a small independent site — this isn't a corporate legal document, just an honest explanation of what happens when you visit.

📅 Updated August 2026
ℹ️ Quick Summary
This site uses Google Analytics to see which pages are useful, contact/quiz forms that go through a third-party form service, and affiliate links (including an automated tool called Travelpayouts Drive) that may earn this site a commission if you book something through them. No visitor data is sold, and form submissions are never shared with advertisers or affiliate partners.
1

Who Runs This Site

Ready? China! (readychinatravel.com) is an independently run site built and maintained by Kat, to help first-time visitors prepare for travel to China. This is not a large company — it's one person's project. If you have a question about your data, use the contact options on the Community page.

2

Analytics — Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are helpful and where visitors are coming from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to collect information such as:

What's collected
  • Pages visited and time spent on them
  • General location (country/city level, from IP address)
  • Device and browser type
  • How you arrived at the site (search engine, link, direct)
What's not collected
  • Your name or contact details (unless you submit a form separately — see below)
  • Anything you type into forms
  • Payment information (this site doesn't process payments)

You can learn more about how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy, and opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3

Contact Forms & the AI Planner

Forms on this site (community submissions, the AI trip planner, and similar features) are handled by Formspree, a third-party form processing service. When you submit a form, the information you type — such as your email address or trip details — is sent directly to Formspree's servers and forwarded to Kat's inbox.

What this means
Anything you submit through a form is used only to respond to you, or, if you've opted into a specific feature, to provide that feature. Form submissions are never passed to Travelpayouts, advertisers, or any affiliate program. This site does not sell or share your contact information with third parties.

You can review Formspree's own data practices at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

4

Affiliate Links & Travelpayouts Drive

Ready? China! is free to use, and is partly funded through affiliate partnerships. When you click certain links on this site (for example, eSIM, flight, hotel, train, VPN, or travel insurance links) and go on to make a booking or purchase, this site may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you.

This site uses Travelpayouts Drive, an automated tool that:

  • Finds existing links to travel brands on this site and routes them through Travelpayouts' tracking system, so referrals can be counted and commissions paid
  • May automatically turn certain travel-related keywords in the text into affiliate links
  • Sets a cookie to record that a booking came from this site, so the referral can be credited — this cookie does not contain your name, email, or other personal details

Other affiliate partners used or planned for this site include Airalo (eSIM), Trip.com (flights/hotels/trains), and travel insurance and VPN providers. Each of these partners has its own privacy and cookie policy governing what happens after you leave this site.

Why this matters to you
Affiliate relationships don't cost you anything extra, and Kat only recommends services actually used or vetted for travel in China. But clicking through to a partner site means that partner's own tracking and privacy policy applies from that point on — this policy only covers what happens on readychinatravel.com itself.
5

Cookies, in Plain Terms

A cookie is a small file your browser stores when you visit a site. This site uses cookies from:

  • Google Analytics — to understand site usage (see Section 2)
  • Travelpayouts — to track affiliate referrals (see Section 4)

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so may affect how affiliate referrals are tracked, but won't stop you from using any part of this site.

6

Your Choices

You can
  • Browse the site without submitting any form — nothing personal is collected unless you type it into a form
  • Block cookies or use browser privacy settings/extensions
  • Ask what data Formspree holds for a submission you made, by contacting Kat directly
Not offered right now
  • A cookie-consent banner (this site currently relies on your browser's own cookie controls)
  • A dedicated account system or data dashboard (there are no user accounts on this site)
