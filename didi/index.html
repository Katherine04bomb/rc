How to Use Didi in China (Setup + Calling a Ride)

Didi is China's Uber. It's cheaper than taxis, safer than flagging random cars, and works entirely in English. Once you have it set up, getting around any Chinese city becomes incredibly easy — even without speaking a single word of Mandarin.

⏱ 10 min setup📅 Updated August 2026✅ Works in English
Setup Guide → How to Call a Ride →
⚠️ Never Get Into an Unlicensed Taxi
At major airports and tourist areas, people will approach you offering a "taxi" or "cheap ride." These are unlicensed drivers who charge 3–5× the normal rate and sometimes take you to the wrong place. Always use Didi — it's tracked, rated, and safe.

  1. 1Download Didi China from App Store or Google Play — search "DiDi" (two capital D's)
  2. 2Tap "Sign Up" and enter your international phone number with country code
  3. 3Enter the SMS code you receive and create a password
  4. 4Go to Me → Wallet → Add Payment Method
  5. 5Add Alipay (recommended) or a foreign credit card directly
  6. 6Set English as your app language: Me → Settings → Language → English
💡 Best Payment Setup
Link your Alipay account to Didi — it's the smoothest. If you haven't set up Alipay yet, do that first. International cards also work directly in Didi but Alipay is more reliable.

Part 2: How to Call a Didi Once You're in China

  1. 1Open Didi and tap the destination field at the top
  2. 2Type your destination in English — Didi finds it (or paste from Amap)
  3. 3Choose your car type — Express is cheapest, Premier is a nicer car
  4. 4Confirm your pickup location by dragging the pin to exactly where you are
  5. 5Tap "Confirm Booking" — a driver accepts within 1–3 minutes
  6. 6Watch the car approach on the map. Check the plate number matches when the car arrives.
  7. 7Payment processes automatically through Alipay when you arrive — no cash needed
💡 The Magic Message — Copy This
If your driver calls you (they sometimes do to confirm pickup location), send this WeChat or SMS message:

我是外国人，不会讲中文。请按照APP上的地址来。
(I'm a foreigner, I don't speak Chinese. Please follow the address in the app.)
Copy and paste — 99% of drivers understand and simply follow the app. Works every time.

Didi Tips & Common Situations

📍 Pickup Problems
  • Pin your exact location on the map or let it auto-detect (it usually shows you your nearest pickup location)
  • At busy areas (malls, stations), walk to the Didi pickup zone marked with 网约车 signs
  • Send your driver a photo of where you're standing if they seem confused
  • If they cancel, just book again — it takes 30 seconds
💰 What Does Didi Cost?
  • Short city ride (5km): ¥15–25 (~$2–4 USD)
  • Airport to city centre: ¥60–120 (~$8–16 USD)
  • Surge pricing applies during rain, rush hour, and public holidays
  • The price depends, but always much cheaper than licensed taxis at airports
🚖 Didi vs Taxi
  • Didi: tracked, rated, English app, payment automatic ✅
  • Taxi: can hail on street, driver may not have app, may not safe
  • Taxi meters are honest — just harder to communicate address
  • Screenshot your hotel address in Chinese to show taxi drivers
⭐ Rating Your Driver (optional)
  • Rate after your trip — some drivers care about their ratings
  • 4.8+ rating = good driver, worth selecting from the list
  • You can also rate a bad experience — Didi platform takes complaints seriously
  • Your passenger rating matters too — be ready on time
