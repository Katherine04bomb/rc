Big Wild Goose Pagoda

🛏 Where to Stay

Sofitel Legend People's Grand
Historic 1950s building inside the city walls

Tang Dynasty themed, near Bell Tower, amazing value

Inside city walls, rooftop with drum tower view

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Rou Jia Mo (肉夹馍)
Chinese 'burger' — spiced braised pork in crispy flatbread
🍽 Yang Rou Pao Mo (羊肉泡馍)
Crumbled bread in lamb soup — Xi'an's soul food
🍽 Biang Biang Noodles
Thick belt noodles with chili oil — the name has 58 strokes!

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Rent a bike and ride the City Wall at sunset — 14km loop, absolutely magical
Kat Says
Muslim Quarter (回民街) is touristy but still worth it — go deeper into the side alleys for better food
Kat Says
Terracotta Army is 1hr from city center. Take tourist bus #306 (游5) from train station — ¥7, not the ¥200 tour bus!
