Dunhuang Night Market — local food & handicrafts

🛏 Where to Stay

The Silk Road Dunhuang Hotel
Upscale hotel with traditional Silk Road decor, closest to Mingsha dunes
Dunhuang Shazhou Hotel
Mid-range, walkable to night market and Shazhou old town
Desert Glamping (Mingsha area)
Sleep under the stars in a luxury desert tent — unforgettable sunrise over the dunes

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Lanzhou Beef Noodles (兰州牛肉面)
Hand-pulled noodles in rich beef broth — Gansu's soul food, available everywhere
🍽 Dunhuang Niang Pi Zi (酿皮子)
Local cold noodle dish with sesame paste and chili oil — refreshing in desert heat
🍽 Lamb Skewers (羊肉串)
Grilled over charcoal with cumin — the night market staple. Pair with cold beer!

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Book Mogao Caves tickets 2+ weeks in advance — they strictly limit daily visitors. The English guided tour is excellent.
Kat Says
Go to Mingsha Mountain at 5pm. The sand cools down, the sunset is magical, and you can slide down the dunes on a wooden board!
Kat Says
Best months: April–June and September–October. Summer is scorching (40°C+), winter is freezing. Bring lip balm and sunglasses — the desert wind is no joke.
