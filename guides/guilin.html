🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Guilin Rice Noodles (桂林米粉)
Breakfast of champions — slippery rice noodles in bone broth, add pickled beans and chili
🍽 Beer Fish (啤酒鱼)
Li River fish braised in beer and tomatoes — Yangshuo specialty
🍽 Luosifen (螺蛳粉)
River snail noodles — pungent, polarizing, and utterly addictive

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Li River cruise: take the 4-hour boat from Guilin to Yangshuo. Bring snacks — the boat food is expensive and mediocre
Kat Says
Yangshuo countryside biking: rent a bike (¥30/day) and get lost in the rice paddies. Best China experience ever
Kat Says
Longji Rice Terraces are magical in May (water-filling season) and October (golden harvest) — avoid winter when they're brown
