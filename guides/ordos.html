🛏 Where to Stay

Mongolian Yurt Camp (Ordos Grassland)
Traditional felt ger with modern bedding — bonfire, starry skies, morning horse rides
Kempinski Hotel Ordos
Luxury in Kangbashi district — surreal modern design, great breakfast, near all attractions
Ordos Dongsheng City Hotel
Practical mid-range in the older city center — close to train station and local restaurants

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Mongolian Roast Whole Lamb (烤全羊)
The centerpiece of any Ordos feast — crispy skin, tender meat, seasoned with wild cumin
🍽 Mongolian Milk Tea (奶茶)
Salty, buttery, and addictive — served in a bowl with millet and dried meat. A meal in itself
🍽 Hand-Grabbed Mutton (手扒肉)
Boiled mutton eaten with your hands — the most authentic Mongolian dining experience

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Best months: July–September. The grasslands are green, temperatures are perfect (20–28°C), and the Naadam Festival happens in July — wrestling, horse racing, archery!
Kat Says
Bring warm clothes even in summer. Ordos is on the Mongolian plateau — nights can drop to 10°C. The wind is relentless, so pack a windbreaker.
Kat Says
Kangbashi is a photographer's dream — massive sculptures, empty boulevards, futuristic buildings. It was built for 1 million people but only 150,000 live there. Truly surreal!
