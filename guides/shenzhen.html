📸 Shenzhen in Photos

🏛 Top 5 Must-See

1
Ping An Finance Centre — 599m observation deck, best city views
2
Huaqiangbei — world's largest electronics market, gadget paradise
3
Dafen Oil Painting Village — watch artists paint, buy affordable originals
4
Dameisha Beach — free public beach, escape the concrete jungle
5
OCT Loft Creative Culture Park — indie cafes, galleries, live music

🛏 Where to Stay

Futian District (CBD)
Best for first-timers — central, walkable to Ping An Tower, Shopping Mall metro hub
Nanshan District
Near Window of the World & OCT Loft, great for culture & nightlife
Luohu District
Budget-friendly, next to Hong Kong border crossing, close to Dongmen food street

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Cantonese Dim Sum (早茶)
Shenzhen's soul — shrimp har gow, siu mai, char siu bao. Go early on weekends!
🍽 Cantonese Roast Goose (烧鹅)
Crispy skin, tender meat — try it at a traditional siu mei shop
🍽 Shajian Chicken (沙井蚝)
Shenzhen's famous local oysters from Shajing — plump, fresh, grilled or steamed

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Shenzhen has a 144-hour visa-free transit policy! If you're flying through China, you can stay up to 6 days without a visa. Just show your onward ticket.
Kat Says
Take the metro everywhere — it's clean, cheap (2-5 RMB), and covers the whole city. Get a Shenzhen Tong card or just use WeChat Pay to scan QR codes at gates.
Kat Says
Best months: October–December. Pleasant 22-28°C, low humidity. Summer (June–Sept) is brutally hot and humid with typhoons. Spring is foggy.
