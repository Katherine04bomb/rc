Hengqin

Chimelong resort, Macau neighbor, entertainment island

✨ Why Hengqin?

Hengqin is a 106-square-km island in the Pearl River Delta, floating just 190 meters from Macau — and home to Chimelong Ocean Kingdom, the Guinness-certified world's largest aquarium, where you can watch an 8-meter whale shark drift past a record-breaking viewing window. It's the family-fun capital of southern China, with a theme-park resort, water parks and nightly fireworks.

The genius of Hengqin is the two-country trick: cross the Lotus Bridge on foot and you're in Macau, eating Portuguese egg tarts and exploring UNESCO-listed streets — two destinations, one trip. Kids under 100cm enter Chimelong free, and the indoor park covers you on rainy days. This is China's easiest, most spectacular family day out.

The story: Opened in 2014, Ocean Kingdom holds 5 Guinness World Records, including the world's largest aquarium — 48.75 million liters of water — and the biggest single tank on earth, a 22.7-million-liter whale-shark exhibit behind a 39.6m × 8.3m acrylic window. Its residents include whale sharks over 8 meters long, manta rays, belugas, polar bears and penguins, spread across 8 themed ocean zones.

Why it's a must-see: This is the closest most people will ever get to a whale shark the size of a bus drifting silently past — plus record-breaking exhibits, a 100km/h roller coaster, a 5D castle theatre and a nightly fireworks spectacular, all in one ticket (adult ¥395, child ¥280).

💡 Kat's tip: Go on a weekday and hit the Whale Shark Aquarium at 10am opening or after 6pm — queues swing from 5 to 45 minutes. Download the Chimelong app for live showtimes. Thrill coasters have height limits (~120cm).

The story: Designed by Populous — the firm behind Wimbledon's Centre Court — and opened in 2015, the center spans ~117,000 m² with a 5,000-seat stadium, a 1,500-seat show court and 16 practice courts. It has hosted the WTA Elite Trophy, the ATP Zhuhai Championship, Davis Cup ties and the 2025 Billie Jean King Cup Finals.

Why it's a must-see: Where else can you stand on the same courts as WTA and ATP pros — then actually book a court and play? In the low season you might watch a world-class match with only a few hundred other fans, which is its own kind of magic.

💡 Kat's tip: Check the event calendar before you book — tournament matches sell out, but practice courts are bookable via the Huafa Sports mini-app most days (open 8am–10pm).

The story: Every night around 8pm, the lake in front of Ocean Kingdom becomes a 15–20-minute stage: over 100 drones, real fireworks, water-jetpack flyers, jet-ski stunts, a dancing music fountain and laser projections that turn the whale-shark building into a giant screen.

Why it's a must-see: It's a full action-movie finale included with your park ticket — part fountain show, part fireworks, part drone display — and the single most photographed moment on the island. Pure spectacle, and the kids will never forget it.

💡 Kat's tip: Claim a lakeside spot ~30 minutes early; the illuminated-float parade leads into the show, and the best angle faces the whale-shark building. Showtimes pause in heavy rain or typhoon alerts.

The story: Hengqin Port runs 24 hours with 'one-stop joint inspection': you queue once and clear both China and Macau, typically in 30 seconds to 20 minutes. Across the Lotus Bridge you're on Macau's Cotai Strip — a ~10-minute taxi from the Venetian-style canals and the UNESCO-listed historic center. Macau's light rail now has a Hengqin Line, making the hop even easier.

Why it's a must-see: It's the world's most effortless two-city day: breakfast in Zhuhai, pastéis de nata in Macau by lunch, back in time for the firework show at night. A genuine two-country adventure that takes zero planning.

💡 Kat's tip: Bring your passport and make sure your China visa permits re-entry (or check China's 240-hour visa-free transit rules). Weekday mornings are fastest at the border; weekends see longer queues.

The story: Hengqin Port was approved for an inbound duty-free shop in January 2026 (up to ¥15,000 of duty-free goods for travelers entering from Macau — check its opening status), while an outbound duty-free shop already operates, and Macau's mega-mall Cotai resorts are 10 minutes away.

Why it's a must-see: It's a shopping loop few tourists find: grab duty-free bargains, stroll through the newest, emptiest border crossing in the Pearl River Delta, and finish with a Portuguese-style photo at the Lopo Boutique plaza, all in one afternoon.

💡 Kat's tip: Duty-free rules depend on your residency and travel frequency — keep receipts and declare what's required. If the new shop isn't open yet, Macau's Cotai malls remain the real retail draw.

🛏 Where to Stay

Pick the right district and half your trip is planned. These are the bases locals actually recommend for visitors.

Chimelong Resort zone Families & park lovers

On-island themed hotels inside the resort: the Penguin Hotel's rooms look into real penguin habitats, Hengqin Bay Hotel adds a 12,000 m² water world and dolphin academy, and the rooftop pool faces the nightly fireworks. You wake up, shuttle 5 minutes, and walk in early.

🚇 Intercity rail + free resort shuttles; ~40 min taxi from central Zhuhai 🏛 Ocean Kingdom, Chimelong Spaceship, circus
e.g. Chimelong Penguin Hotel (~900–1200 RMB) · Hengqin Bay Hotel (~825+ RMB) · combo deals with park tickets
Novotown (Innovation Square) Rainy-day families & mall convenience

A mixed-use complex of shopping, cinema and indoor family fun — trampoline arenas, indoor karting, arcades — anchored by the 493-room Hyatt Regency. The perfect wet-weather base, ~10 minutes from both the border and the park.

🚇 Free shuttles to Hengqin Port and the HK-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge port 🏛 Novotown mall, Hengqin Port, Cotai (Macau)
e.g. Hyatt Regency Hengqin (~571–800 RMB) with family-themed 'space' and 'animal' rooms
Hengqin Port & Finance Island Macau day-trippers

The closest hotels to the border and Macau's light-rail station — perfect if Macau is the priority and the park is a day trip. Evenings glow with the Portuguese-style Lopo plaza and the IFC tower across the water from Cotai.

🚇 Walk to Hengqin Port (24h); LRT Hengqin Line to Macau ~2 min 🏛 Lotus Bridge, Cotai casinos, Hengqin Port
e.g. Artyzen Habitat Zhuhai (~450 RMB) · modern serviced apartments (~400 RMB)
Shenjing Village Backpackers & budget foodies

A fishing village 3.8 km from Ocean Kingdom where family homestays start around ¥180/night — a tenth of the on-resort price. The streets below are Hengqin's best cheap-food lane: charcoal-grilled oysters and seafood hotpots.

🚇 One bus stop or ~10 min taxi to the park; ~15 min to the port 🏛 Ocean Kingdom, Flower Sea Corridor promenade
e.g. local guesthouses & ocean-themed homestays ¥180–600

🍜 What to Eat

Charcoal-Grilled Hengqin Oysters 炭烧横琴蚝

Hengqin's signature fat oysters, grilled over charcoal with garlic — sweet, briny and cheap. The island's name is practically synonymous with oysters.

🍽 🍽 Shenjing Village street stalls (~¥70 for 6) · Hao Daxian at Novotown
Oyster & Chicken Hotpot 生蚝鸡火锅

A bubbling local pot where plump oysters poach into a rich chicken broth — the classic Hengqin comfort meal.

🍽 🍽 Hao Xuan Ge pressure-cooker oyster hotpot · ~¥80–90 pp
Cantonese Dim Sum 粤式早茶

Har gow and siu mai straight from the steamer — the classic Pearl River Delta breakfast ritual, done properly here.

🍽 🍽 Yijian Har Gow King (益健虾饺皇) in town · ¥60–100 pp
Macau-Style Egg Tart 葡式蛋挞

Blistering caramelized top, flaky crust — you're 20 minutes from the best egg tarts on earth. Cross the bridge and taste the difference.

🍽 🍽 Macau (20 min away) — Lord Stow's or Margaret's · ~¥10 per tart

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
