🛏 Where to Stay

Chimelong Penguin Hotel
Right inside the resort — wake up to penguins outside your window. Kids go absolutely nuts
Chimelong Hengqin Bay Hotel
Beachfront luxury with dolphin-themed rooms, ocean views, and direct park access
Hyatt Regency Hengqin
Modern business-leisure hotel near the port — great if you're splitting time with Macau

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Cantonese Dim Sum (点心)
Steamed shrimp dumplings, char siu bao, egg tarts — Hengqin does Cantonese food right
🍽 Fresh Seafood Hotpot
Zhuhai is famous for seafood — pick your fish from the tank, hotpot it tableside
🍽 Macau-Style Portuguese Egg Tart
Blistering caramelized top, flaky crust — you're 20 minutes from the best egg tarts on earth

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Hengqin + Macau is the ultimate 2-in-1 trip. Spend day 1 at Ocean Kingdom, cross the Lotus Bridge, and spend day 2 eating egg tarts and exploring Macau's old town. You'll need a visa for both!
Kat Says
Chimelong gets CROWDED on weekends and Chinese holidays. Go on a weekday — lines drop from 2 hours to 15 minutes. Book tickets online for a discount.
Kat Says
Best months: October–April. Summer (June–September) is hot, humid, and typhoon season. Check the weather before you book!
