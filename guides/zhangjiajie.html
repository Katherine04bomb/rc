🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Tujia San Xia Guo (三下锅)
Zhangjiajie's signature hotpot — three meats (usually pork, beef, lamb) in a spicy broth. Order it everywhere
🍽 Xiangxi Smoked Bacon (湘西腊肉)
Hunan-style smoked pork belly — smoky, salty, slightly sweet. Stir-fried with garlic shoots = perfection
🍽 Sour Fish Soup (酸汤鱼)
Tangy, spicy freshwater fish soup — a Tujia minority specialty that warms you after a long day of hiking

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Buy the 4-day park pass (¥228). The park is HUGE — you need at least 2 full days to see the highlights. One day is not enough, trust me.
Kat Says
AVOID Chinese national holidays at all costs. Golden Week (Oct 1–7) and Spring Festival turn the glass bridge into a human parking lot. Go in April, May, or September instead.
Kat Says
Enter the park at 7am when gates open. By 10am the tour buses arrive and the queues get biblical. Early morning mist between the pillars is the most magical — and most photogenic — time.
