🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Oyster Omelette (蚵仔煎)
Crispy outside, gooey inside — the ultimate Minnan street food
🍽 Satay Noodles (沙茶面)
Peanut-sesame broth noodles with seafood — Quanzhou's signature comfort food
🍽 Runbing (润饼)
Fresh spring rolls with everything — sweet, savory, crunchy, soft all at once

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Quanzhou is off the typical foreign tourist radar — very little English spoken. Download Pleco (Chinese dictionary app) before you come
Kat Says
Zhongshan Road at night is the most beautiful pedestrian street in southern China — go after 7pm
Kat Says
Xiamen is only 1 hour away by high-speed train — perfect combo trip. Gulangyu Island is a must
