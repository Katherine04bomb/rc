Naxi culture, cobblestone old town, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain

✨ Why Lijiang?

Lijiang is where China's old town charm meets its highest drama: a 800-year-old cobblestone town of canals and wooden houses (UNESCO World Heritage since 1997), set against the 5,596-meter Jade Dragon Snow Mountain rising right behind it. It's the home of the Naxi people and their Dongba culture — the last living pictographic script on earth.

By day you can ride a cable car to 4,506 meters and stand on a glacier; by night the old town's bars and bonfires take over. It's the perfect blend of culture, mountain adventure and nightlife — and one of the most photographed corners of China.

The story: Lijiang Old Town was founded in the late Song dynasty (13th century) and built without walls — a Naxi tradition, since a city with walls 'traps' good fortune. Its cobblestone lanes follow the canals of the Jade River, which flows through every street (the Naxi used to wash the town's stone by opening sluice gates). It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997, the first old town in China to be listed.

Why it's a must-see: There's no other old town quite like it: waterways threading every lane, stone bridges every few dozen meters, and a layout designed to be walked without ever feeling lost. It's also genuinely alive — residents still live and trade here, and the surrounding Naxi courtyards (with their 'three rooms one screen wall' architecture) are beautiful.

💡 Kat's tip: Wander off the main tourist lanes (Sifang Street) into the quieter northern alleys, especially at 7am when the town is empty and the canals sing. Entry is free, but there's a 50 RMB 'maintenance fee' collected at some gates — it's included if you stay in a licensed guesthouse.

The story: Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (Yulong Xueshan) is a 13-peak range whose highest summit reaches 5,596 meters, permanently snow-capped and towering 4,000 meters above Lijiang's valley floor. The Glacier Park cable car climbs to 4,506 meters — among the highest cable car stations in Asia — putting you on a real glacier at the edge of the clouds.

Why it's a must-see: The contrast is the magic: subtropical valley one moment, glacier and thin air the next. The ride up is a show of its own, and at the top you can walk a boardwalk onto the glacier itself. Below, the Impression Lijiang show (by the director of Avatar's sound designer, Zhang Yimou's team) sets 500 performers against the mountain.

💡 Kat's tip: Book the cable car ticket online (peak season sells out days ahead; ~140 RMB + entrance 100 RMB). Bring oxygen (30 RMB at the base) even if you feel fine — altitude hits fast. Go early; clouds roll in by noon.

The story: Black Dragon Pool (Heilongtan) is a spring-fed lake at the north edge of the old town, designed as a Ming-dynasty park with the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain as its backdrop. The five-arch Jade Dragon Bridge and the Deyue Tower pagoda sit in the middle of the pool, and on clear days the entire snow mountain range is reflected in the still water — the most photographed view in Yunnan.

Why it's a must-see: It's the postcard shot: pagoda, bridge and snow mountain in a single frame of mirror-still water. The park is also home to the Dongba Museum, where you can see the Naxi's pictographic script — the world's last living pictographic writing system — up close.

💡 Kat's tip: Go in the early morning for the calmest water and the clearest reflection; by 10am the wind picks up and the mirror ripples. Entry 50 RMB (some hotels include it). The Dongba Museum inside is worth 30 minutes.

The story: The Naxi people's Dongba culture is one of the world's great survivals: a religion and script — over 1,400 pictographic characters — that has been passed down by Dongba priests for over 1,000 years and is still in use today. The Dongba Culture Research Institute in Lijiang preserves it, and cultural shows around the old town stage the songs, dances and rituals that go with it.

Why it's a must-see: You can watch an actual living pictographic script being read, and see ritual dance and music that descend from shamanic traditions older than the town itself. It's a rare chance to meet a culture that has no equivalent anywhere else on earth.

💡 Kat's tip: Visit the Dongba Museum at Black Dragon Pool first (free with the 50 RMB park entry), then catch an evening Naxi music and dance show in the old town (from ~100 RMB). The ancient music ensemble — with 500-year-old instruments — is the unmissable one.

The story: When the sun sets, the old town's riverside lanes turn into Yunnan's most famous nightlife strip: bars with live bands playing everything from folk to rock, bonfires where locals and travelers join hands to dance, and the canal reflections doubling every light. The main 'Bar Street' along the river is the energetic core; the side lanes hide quieter, cooler spots.

Why it's a must-see: It's the yin to the mountain's yang: after a day of glaciers and temples, Lijiang lets loose. The bonfire circle dances (everyone holding hands, stepping in a circle to Naxi music) are joyful and unpretentious — you'll be pulled in whether you like it or not.

💡 Kat's tip: Drinks cost double on the main Bar Street — follow the music into the side lanes for the same scene at half the price. The bonfire dance starts around 8:30pm in the main square. Be careful with the cobblestones after a couple of drinks!

🛏 Where to Stay

Pick the right district and half your trip is planned. These are the bases locals actually recommend for visitors.

Inside the Old Town Atmosphere & romance

Stay in a restored Naxi courtyard house — wooden balconies, fish ponds and moon gates — right in the cobblestone maze. Magical at night when the crowds thin, but pack light: luggage has to be carried over cobblestones (some guesthouses offer pickup).

🚇 No metro — walk or taxi to the old town edge 🏛 Sifang Street, canals, Bar Street
e.g. Blossom Hill Lijiang (boutique courtyard) · hundreds of courtyard guesthouses from ¥200
Shuhe Ancient Town Quieter & more authentic

A smaller, quieter old town 20 minutes north of Lijiang — the same cobblestone-and-canal charm without the crowds, plus better-value guesthouses. Perfect for travelers who want the old-town experience without the party.

🚇 No metro — bus or taxi from Lijiang (20 min) 🏛 Shuhe old town, Green Dragon Bridge, Jade Dragon views
e.g. boutique courtyard hotels in Shuhe from ¥250
New town (around the old town) Convenience & budget

The modern city ring around the old town: standard hotels, easy luggage access and cheaper prices, 10 minutes' walk from the old town gates. Best if you're on a budget or arriving by plane with big bags.

🚇 No metro — walking distance to old town 🏛 Old town gates, bus station, restaurants
e.g. InterContinental Lijiang (luxury at the mountain foot) · budget chains in the new town
Ganhaizi / Jade Dragon foothills Mountain views

Hotels and resorts on the meadow at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain — waking up to the full mountain wall outside your window. 30 minutes from the old town; you'll taxi for meals and nightlife.

🚇 No metro — taxi to old town ~30 min 🏛 Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, Impression Lijiang show
e.g. Banyan Tree Lijiang (mountain-view villas, splurge)

🍜 What to Eat

Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles 过桥米线

Yunnan's most famous dish: a bowl of boiling-hot broth you cook raw toppings in at the table — sliced meat, vegetables, quail egg, and rice noodles. Named for the legend of a wife crossing a bridge to deliver it to her husband.

🍽 🍽 Old town noodle houses · ¥30–60 per set
Naxi Baba 纳西粑粑

Pan-fried flatbread — sweet (with sugar and nuts) or savory (with ham and scallion) — the classic Naxi street snack, crisp on the outside, soft within.

🍽 🍽 Street stalls across the old town · ¥10–15
Yak Meat Hotpot 牦牛肉火锅

Tender highland yak slices simmered in a spicy broth with mushrooms and greens — hearty, warming, and perfect after a cold mountain day.

🍽 🍽 Old town hotpot restaurants · ¥80–120 pp
Lijiang Mushroom Hotpot 野生菌火锅

Yunnan is China's mushroom kingdom — a hotpot of foraged wild mushrooms (porcini, matsutake and more) in chicken broth. Seasonal (June–Oct) and unforgettable.

🍽 🍽 Mushroom restaurants in the old town · ¥100–150 pp

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Lijiang sits at 2,400m — give yourself a day to adjust: drink lots of water, walk slowly, and skip the first-night bar crawl. The mountain cable car is another 2,000m up, so bring oxygen if you're prone to altitude sickness.
Kat Says
The old town is packed during Chinese holidays (Golden Week, Spring Festival). Go in April, May or October for the best balance of weather and crowds.
Kat Says
Wear proper walking shoes — it's all cobblestones, and you'll walk a LOT. High heels = regret. And carry your guesthouse's card with you; the lanes look identical after dark.
