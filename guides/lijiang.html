Lijiang Old Town — UNESCO cobblestone maze

Black Dragon Pool — iconic reflection of Snow Mountain
4
Naxi Dongba culture show — ancient pictographic script

🛏 Where to Stay

Blossom Hill Lijiang (花间堂)
Boutique Naxi courtyard inn right in the old town — stunning, romantic, authentic
InterContinental Lijiang
Luxury resort at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, with spa & mountain views
Lijiang Old Town Youth Hostel
Budget pick in a traditional courtyard, great for meeting other travelers

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles (过桥米线)
Yunnan's most famous dish — hot broth, raw toppings you cook at the table
🍽 Naxi Baba (纳西粑粑)
Pan-fried flatbread — sweet or savory. A street food you'll find everywhere in the old town
🍽 Yak Meat Hotpot
Hearty highland specialty — tender yak slices in spicy broth, perfect after a cold mountain day

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Lijiang is at 2,400m altitude. Give yourself a day to adjust — drink lots of water, take it slow, skip the booze on day one.
Kat Says
The old town gets PACKED during Chinese holidays (Golden Week, Spring Festival). Go in shoulder season (April, October) for the best experience.
Kat Says
Wear comfortable walking shoes! The old town is all cobblestones, and you'll be walking a LOT. High heels = regret.
