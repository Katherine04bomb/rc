🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Dongpo Pork (东坡肉)
Melt-in-your-mouth braised pork belly — Hangzhou's most famous dish
🍽 Longjing Shrimp (龙井虾仁)
Freshwater shrimp stir-fried with Dragon Well tea leaves — elegant and unique
🍽 West Lake Vinegar Fish (西湖醋鱼)
Sweet-sour grass carp — a Hangzhou classic that's an acquired taste but worth trying

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
West Lake is huge — rent an e-bike (¥30/hr) to circle the entire lake. Walking takes 4+ hours
Kat Says
Longjing tea village: go in spring (March-April) for tea picking season. You can pick, roast, and drink your own tea
Kat Says
Suzhou is 30 minutes away by high-speed train — world-famous classical gardens. Highly recommend a day trip
