Guangzhou

Cantonese culture, dim sum capital, Pearl River nights

★★☆☆☆ 🗓 2–3 days FoodieHistoryRiverside

📸 Guangzhou in Photos

🏛 Top 5 Must-See

1
Canton Tower — 600m icon with sky drop ride & observation decks
2
Shamian Island — colonial-era buildings, banyan trees, riverside walks
3
Morning Yum Cha (饮早茶) — the real Cantonese dim sum experience
4
Pearl River Night Cruise — see the city skyline lit up from the water
5
Chen Clan Ancestral Hall — exquisite Lingnan craftsmanship & folk art museum

🛏 Where to Stay

Zhujiang New Town (Tianhe)
Modern CBD near Canton Tower, best for first-timers, great metro access
Shamian / Liwan District
Old Cantonese charm, walkable to colonial architecture & traditional food streets
Beijing Road Area
Shopping central, pedestrian street, budget to mid-range options

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Cantonese Dim Sum (点心)
Guangzhou invented it — har gow, siu mai, cheong fun, egg tarts. Go before 11am!
🍽 Wonton Noodles (云吞面)
Thin egg noodles with shrimp wontons in clear broth — simple but perfect
🍽 Roast Suckling Pig (烧乳猪)
Guangzhou's showstopper — crispy skin, tender meat, usually pre-ordered at restaurants

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
For authentic yum cha, go to a traditional restaurant like Tao Tao Ju (陶陶居) or Lianxianglou (莲香楼). Arrive before 10am on weekends or expect a 1-hour queue.
Kat Says
Canton Tower is best at sunset — go up 30 min before dark to see the city in daylight, then watch it light up. The sky drop ride at the top is not for the faint-hearted!
Kat Says
Best months: November–February. Mild and dry, perfect walking weather. Summer (June–Sept) is hot, humid, and rainy. Avoid typhoon season in August.
