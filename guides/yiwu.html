Crispy caramel-coated hawthorn berries on a stick — the ultimate market snack
🍽 Donghe Meat Pie (东河肉饼)
Yiwu's signature — thin, crispy pastry stuffed with seasoned pork. Find it at any street stall

Freshwater fish in a light soy-based sauce — Zhejiang cuisine at its cleanest, most delicate best

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
The Trade City has 5 districts spread over 4 million sqm. You CANNOT see it all in one day. Research which district has what you want BEFORE you go (toys=District 1, jewelry=District 2, electronics=District 2F).
Kat Says
Most booths sell wholesale only (MOQ = minimum order quantity). But many will sell you a sample piece if you ask nicely. Bring cash — small vendors often prefer it.
Kat Says
Yiwu is only 1.5 hours from Hangzhou by high-speed train. Combine it with a Hangzhou trip — shop in Yiwu, relax at West Lake the next day!
