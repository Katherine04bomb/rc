🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Lamb Kebabs (烤肉)
Chargrilled over charcoal with cumin and chili — you'll smell them from three blocks away
🍽 Hand-Pulled Noodles (拉条子)
Thick, chewy noodles with lamb and peppers — a Uyghur staple you'll dream about after
🍽 Polo (抓饭 — Pilaf)
Fragrant rice cooked with lamb, carrots, and raisins — Central Asia on a plate

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
The Sunday livestock market is an absolute MUST. Go before 10am — that's when the donkey-and-sheep trading action peaks. It's unlike anything you've seen.
Kat Says
Kashgar is ★★★★★ difficulty for a reason. English is rare, Uyghur and Mandarin dominate. Download Pleco (Chinese dictionary) and a translation app offline before you go.
Kat Says
Bring cash! Many small shops and food stalls in the old city don't take WeChat Pay from foreign cards. RMB cash is still king in the bazaar.
