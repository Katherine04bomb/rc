🛏 Where to Stay

Tea Plantation B&B (茶园民宿)
Wake up overlooking misty tea terraces — breakfast is fresh-picked tea and farm eggs
Taihu Lake Resort (太湖度假村)
Lakeside resort 30min from Changxing — spa, lake views, seafood dinners on the water
Changxing County Hotel
Clean, modern, and central — perfect base if you want comfort without the fuss

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Changxing Purple Shoot Tea (紫笋茶)
One of China's top 10 teas — delicate, floral, grown right here. Buy it from the plantation
🍽 Bamboo Shoot Dishes (笋)
Spring bamboo shoots braised, stir-fried, or in soup — Changxing's bamboo forests are legendary
🍽 Taihu Lake Whitebait (太湖银鱼)
Tiny translucent fish scrambled with eggs — a Zhejiang delicacy you won't find outside the region

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Changxing is the anti-tourist destination. No crowds, no ticket queues, just quiet tea fields and friendly farmers. Perfect if you need a breather from China's intense cities.
Kat Says
Best time: March–May for tea-picking season and bamboo shoots. October–November for autumn colors. The tea terraces turn golden — stunning.
Kat Says
Changxing is 1 hour from Hangzhou by car, 2 hours from Shanghai. It makes a perfect overnight side trip — leave the city bustle behind and wake up in a tea field.
