🏛 Top 5 Must-See

The Bund — colonial buildings facing the futuristic skyline
2
Yu Garden — classical Chinese garden in the city
3
French Concession — cafés, trees, architecture
4
Shanghai Museum — free, world-class
5
Tianzifang — artsy alleyway with shops

🛏 Where to Stay

The Puli Hotel (Jing'an)
Modern luxury, central, walking distance to French Concession
Casa Serena Boutique Hotel
1920s Spanish villa in French Concession, very charming
Mingtown Etour Youth Hostel
Budget option on the Bund, rooftop view of the skyline

🍜 What to Eat

🍽 Xiaolongbao at Jia Jia Tang Bao
Michelin-mentioned soup dumplings, not fancy but legendary
🍽 Shengjian at Da Hu Chun
Pan-fried pork buns — crispy bottom, juicy inside, pure joy
🍽 Hairy crab (Oct-Nov only)
Seasonal delicacy — locals go crazy for it. Worth planning around!

💡 Kat's Insider Tips

Kat Says
Get a Shanghai Metro card — refundable deposit, works on buses too
Kat Says
The Bund at night and in the morning are two completely different experiences — do both
Kat Says
French Concession is best explored by bike. Rent a shared bike (Meituan/Hellobike) for ¥1.5
