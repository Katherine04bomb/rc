VPN for China: What's Blocked and How to Stay Connected

China's internet is different from the rest of the world. Google, Instagram, WhatsApp, and hundreds of other sites are blocked. A VPN lets you use them normally — but there's one critical rule: download and test it BEFORE you land in China.

⏱ 8 min read📅 Updated August 2026✅ Tested in China
🚨 The Most Important Rule
You cannot download VPN apps from the App Store or Google Play once you're inside China — they're removed from the Chinese versions of these stores. Download, install, and TEST your VPN at home before your flight. This is the number one mistake first-time visitors make.

💡 Honest Reality
Most of your day-to-day China life (maps, payments, food, transport) uses Chinese apps that work perfectly without a VPN. You'll only miss Instagram, WhatsApp, and Google. Once you're set up with the right apps, it's genuinely fine.
2

Which VPN Actually Works in China?

Free VPNs almost never work reliably in China. The firewall actively blocks VPN traffic and free services don't have the resources to stay ahead. Invest in a paid VPN — it costs less than one coffee per month.

⚠️ On Legality
VPN use by tourists in China is a legal grey area. Millions of foreign visitors use them every year without any issues. The Chinese government's enforcement focuses on VPN providers and businesses, not individual tourists using them for personal use. Use it sensibly — for personal browsing, not anything that would draw attention.
3

How to Set Up Your VPN Before Flying

  1. 1Choose a VPN (ExpressVPN or NordVPN recommended) and sign up on their website
  2. 2Download the app on ALL your devices — phone, tablet, laptop
  3. 3Connect to a Hong Kong or Japan server to test — these are fastest from China
  4. 4Open Google, Instagram, YouTube — confirm everything works
  5. 5Look for the VPN's "Stealth" or "Obfuscated" mode — enable it. This makes VPN traffic harder for the firewall to detect.
  6. 6In China: connect to VPN → then open your apps. That's it.
💡 Backup Plan
Download 2–3 VPN apps. The firewall occasionally blocks specific VPN servers. If one stops working, switch to another. Having a backup takes 5 minutes now and saves a lot of frustration later.
