China Immigration & Arrival: What to Expect Step by Step

Landing in China for the first time feels big. The airport signs are in Chinese, there are queues everywhere, and you're jet-lagged. Here's exactly what happens from the moment the plane lands to the moment you're in a taxi to your hotel — so there are no surprises.

⏱ 7 min read📅 Updated August 2026✅ Step-by-step with no gaps
💡 Pre-Fill Your Arrival Card Online
China now lets you fill in your Arrival Card online up to 72 hours before landing — search "中国入境健康申报" or ask your airline. Skip the paper form on the plane and go straight to immigration. Huge time-saver.
1

On the Plane (Before Landing)

  1. 1Flight attendants may distribute an Arrival Card (入境卡) — fill it in if given one (name, passport number, flight number, hotel address in China, purpose of visit)
  2. 2Have your hotel address in Chinese ready — immigration may ask for it
  3. 3Have your return or onward flight information accessible
  4. 4Switch your phone to the China eSIM if you have one — it activates when you land
  5. 5Keep your passport in your hand — you'll need it multiple times in the next hour
2

Immigration (入境检查)

  1. 1Follow signs to 入境 (Immigration) — usually very well marked in English too
  2. 2Join the 外国人 (Foreigners) queue — separate from Chinese citizens
  3. 3At the booth, hand over your passport + Arrival Card
  4. 4The officer will take your fingerprints and a photo — completely routine, don't be nervous
  5. 5If asked questions, keep answers simple: purpose = tourism (旅游 lǚ yóu) (or whatever your purpose is), duration = your planned stay
  6. 6They stamp your passport and hand it back — you're in China! YAYYY 🇨🇳
💡 What the Officer Might Ask
"What is the purpose of your visit?" → "Tourism/Business trip/Study/Visit" /
"How long will you stay?" → "[X] days"
"Where are you staying?" → Show your hotel booking on your phone

Officers are professional and used to 'foreigners'. Relax — this takes under 5 minutes.
3

Customs Declaration (海关申报)

✅ You Can Bring
  • Up to ¥5,000 RMB cash without declaring
  • Up to $5,000 USD equivalent in foreign currency
  • Personal electronics (phone, laptop, camera)
  • Power banks (must be under 20,000mAh and has a 3C (CCC) sign — check yours)
  • Prescription medication (bring doctor's note)
  • 200 cigarettes or 50 cigars
  • 1.5 litres of alcohol
❌ Do NOT Bring
  • Fresh fruit, vegetables, or meat products
  • Dairy products
  • More than ¥20,000 RMB cash without declaring
  • Weapons or anything that looks like one
  • Content that could be deemed politically sensitive
  • Power banks over 20,000mAh or 100Wh

Most travelers take the green "Nothing to Declare" channel. If you're unsure, use the red channel and ask — officers are helpful.

💡 Fresh Food Rule
The "no fresh food" rule is strictly enforced. That banana you were eating on the plane? Finish it or throw it away before landing. Customs dogs are excellent and fines for undeclared food items start at ¥2,000.
4

Baggage Claim & Arrivals Hall

  1. 1Follow signs to 行李提取 (Baggage Claim)
  2. 2Check the departure board for your flight number and belt number
  3. 3If your bag is lost or damaged: report it at the airline desk before leaving the baggage area — after you leave, claims become much harder
  4. 4Exit through customs (green or red channel) into the Arrivals Hall
  5. 5You're out! Now: ignore taxi touts (there are lots od spam), find the SIM counter if needed, then get your Didi or airport metro
⚠️ First 5 Minutes Outside Arrivals
People WILL approach you offering taxis, rides, or help with luggage. They are not official. Better say "no thanks," and keep walking. Get your phone out, open Didi, and book your ride from the official ride-hailing zone (网约车区) — all airports have one, follow the signs.
5

Your First Hour Priority List

1
SIM / eSIM
Activate your eSIM or buy a SIM — you need data NOW
2
VPN
Connect to your VPN to access Instagram, WhatsApp etc.
3
Didi
Book your ride to the hotel from the 网约车 zone
4
Hotel
Check in, get their Chinese address card, ask and connect to Wi-Fi
