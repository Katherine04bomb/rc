Outlook works in China — no VPN needed

📅 2026-11-16 CHINA NEWS
📧 Outlook works in China — no VPN needed Outlook went down worldwide a few days ago — and that is exactly why this one matters for your trip. **THE TRAP** Your flight, hotel and ticket confirmations all live in your inbox. If the only way you can reach them in China is through a VPN, and the VPN will not connect on hotel Wi-Fi — or you never set it up before you landed — those confirmations are out of reach right when you need them most. **THE FIX** Outlook is not blocked on mainland China networks. It opens on hotel Wi-Fi and on a local SIM with no VPN needed. Before you fly, move your travel bookings to an Outlook address — forward the confirmations while you are still at home — sign in once, and check they are there. Then you can open your inbox on arrival like normal. **ONE MORE TRAP** That outage is the exception that proves the rule: it was on Microsoft's side, not a China block — and it still left people locked out of their inboxes for hours. And Outlook can be slow in China at busy times, so do not rely on a live inbox at the gate — screenshot your tickets and save offline copies before you leave the hotel. — Kat
