12306 seat choice is not always random — check for the seat map before you pay

📅 2026-11-15 CHINA NEWS
🚆 12306 seat choice is not always random — check for the seat map before you pay You booked your train in English and got the seat you did not want — not bad luck, just the English version. **THE TRAP** The English version used to assign your seat at random, and on some trains it still does. But it now shows a Select Seat grid on many high-speed trains — so "English = no choice" is no longer true for every train. **THE FIX** At checkout, look for "Select Seat" (选座) before you pay. If the grid is there, tap A or F for a window, C or D for an aisle (B is the middle seat). If it is greyed out or missing, switch to the Chinese version (标准版) in Settings — that still shows the preference most reliably. Either way it is a request, not a guarantee: if your preferred spot is gone, the system assigns one at random. **ONE MORE TRAP** One traveller reported the payment button stalling on 12306 at the last step. If it happens to you, do not keep tapping it — try Trip.com as a backup (check the fee line at checkout, it varies) or use browser translation on the Chinese site instead. — Kat
