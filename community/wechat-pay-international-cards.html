WeChat Pay expands international card support — now covers 80% of foreign visitors

📅 3 August 2026 CHINA NEWS
Good news if you prefer WeChat over Alipay: WeChat Pay now accepts international cards from 7 major networks — Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Discover, Diners Club, and UnionPay International — covering most foreign visitors to China. Last verified: 20 October 2026. The fee rules (straight from WeChat Pay's official page): - Single transactions of ¥200 or less: NO handling fee. - Single transactions over ¥200: a handling fee applies (currently 3% — verify in-app before you rely on it). - Promo until 31 December 2026: within 90 days of your first international-card transaction, you get an extra ¥1,000 per day of fee-free spending. One-time offer per account, so activate it early in your trip, not at the end. How to bind an international card: 1. Open WeChat → "Me" → "Services" → "Wallet" 2. Tap "Bank Cards" → "Add a Card" 3. Enter your card number, expiry date and CVV 4. Confirm the SMS code your bank sends — that's how WeChat verifies the card 5. Done. The card logo appears in your wallet and you can scan any QR code. Worth knowing before you go: - Some US-issued cards get rejected at the binding step. If yours fails, try a different card — it's the bank, not you. - Set up WeChat Pay AND Alipay. Between them they cover virtually every payment scenario; some merchants only accept one. - For the full setup walkthrough including real-name verification, see our Alipay guide — the card binding flow is nearly identical. The honest take: WeChat Pay's international support is now genuinely usable for tourists, and the fee-free promo makes short trips essentially free of payment fees. But Alipay still has the smoother foreigner onboarding — set up both and you won't get stuck anywhere.
