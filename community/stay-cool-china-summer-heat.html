How to stay cool in China's August heat without spending a cent

📅 August 2026 KAT'S TIP
August is brutal. But there are secret ways to stay cool for free: (1) Big shopping malls blast AC all day and have plenty of seating — MixC and Joy City chains are everywhere. (2) Most museums are heavily air-conditioned and entry is often free or under ¥30. (3) Public libraries are another hidden gem — quiet, freezing cold, and free WiFi. (4) Starbucks in China has AC and you can sit for hours without buying a second drink. Just don't plan outdoor sightseeing between 11am and 3pm and you'll survive.
